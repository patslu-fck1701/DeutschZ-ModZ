[h1]🖥️ DEUTSCHZ MENÜZ — MAIN MENU, LOADING SCREEN & MUSIC[/h1]

[b]Your DeutschZ experience starts before you enter Chernarus.[/b]

DeutschZ MenüZ delivers a dark survival main menu, rotating loading screens, server information, event notices and atmospheric music in one client-and-server mod.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]🔥 FEATURES[/h2]

[list]
[*] custom dark-glass DeutschZ main menu
[*] server status, news, event cards and community links
[*] three rotating loading-screen artworks
[*] random rules, tips and event hints
[*] eleven integrated menu music tracks
[*] no spatial speaker objects and no trader speakers
[*] custom loading, death and respawn interfaces
[*] server-prepared DeutschZ spawn selection
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]🎵 MUSIC WITHOUT SPEAKER SPAM[/h2]

Music is played directly by the menu/event UI and follows normal DayZ audio settings. The mod does not create phone speakers, trader speakers or additional loudspeaker objects in the world.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]🧩 CORE OR STANDALONE[/h2]

MenüZ is included in [b]@DeutschZ_only_core[/b] and is also available as [b]@DeutschZ_MenuMusic[/b]. Do not load both copies of the same PBO.
