[h1]💰 DEUTSCHZ CRIMINALZ — ATM ROBBERIES & CITY STORE NETWORK[/h1]

[b]Hack the ATM. Hold the action for several minutes. Survive the alarm, the red marker and hostile players. Secure NoxZ Euro only.[/b]

DeutschZ CriminalZ turns ATMs and locked vehicles into server-authoritative risk activities. Every supported City Store automatically receives a functional Expansion ATM and a custom DeutschZ information sign without maintaining a world-coordinate list.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]🏪 AUTOMATIC CITY STORE NETWORK[/h2]

ATM and sign positions are calculated from each store's local model transform. Rotated stores and stores created later by a map loader are supported. Matching objects inside the configurable 0.75-metre duplicate radius are reused and corrected; unrelated objects are never removed.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]🚗 VEHICLES & GARAGES[/h2]

Vehicle unlocking uses the locally verified ExpansionVehicle.Get, IsLocked and ForceUnlock API. The general Garage unlock module remains disabled because no safe universal Garage API has been verified for the installed Expansion version.
