[h2]🎮 EVENT FLOW[/h2]

[b]1. LOCATE THE CRASHSITE[/b]

The event announces the attacked convoy and marks its search area. RussianZ and AmericanZ are already fighting; approaching players become targets too.

[b]2. SECURE THE COMBAT ZONE[/b]

Three lightly equipped Expansion AI form each initial faction. Their configured accuracy is [b]0.03 to 0.12[/b].

[b]3. HACK THE BLACKBOX FOR 90 SECONDS[/b]

The action must be held continuously. Death, unconsciousness, excessive distance, an invalid target or releasing the action cancels the hack and resets progress.

[b]4. EXTRACT THE DATACORE[/b]

After a successful hack, the blackbox creates only [b]DZBBC_DataCore[/b]. The recovery team may spawn only now. The BattlegroundZ keycard never comes from ConvoyZ; it comes from the KotHZ boss remains.

[b]5. REACH THE TERMINAL[/b]

Only the owner of the exact DataCore entity can start an active military terminal. Decryption must be held uninterrupted for [b]60 seconds[/b].

[b]6. FINAL DEFENSE & REWARD[/b]

Successful decryption triggers the final defense. The server releases the reward only after a valid event completion and then performs controlled cleanup.
