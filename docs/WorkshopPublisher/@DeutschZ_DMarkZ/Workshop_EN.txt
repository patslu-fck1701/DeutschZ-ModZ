[h1]💶 DEUTSCHZ D-MARKZ — THE OFFICIAL DEUTSCHZ CURRENCY[/h1]

[b]Nine denominations. One economy. One unmistakable DeutschZ identity.[/b]

DeutschZ D-MarkZ replaces the visible NoxZ Euro coin and banknote designs with a fictional DeutschZ currency. The classes remain compatible with NoxZ Phone, ATMs, traders and CriminalZ while names and textures carry the DeutschZ D-MarkZ identity.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]🏧 ECONOMY & CRIMINALZ[/h2]

CriminalZ technically continues to pay through the NoxZ Euro classes. When DeutschZ D-MarkZ is loaded, those classes appear with the DeutschZ coin and banknote visuals. This preserves the existing banking and trader system without introducing a competing currency backend.

Bitcoin is not replaced by D-MarkZ and is not part of this mod.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]🛡️ SERVER AUTHORITY & CONFIGURATION[/h2]

This mod supplies item classes and textures. Balances, payouts, purchases, sales and rewards remain controlled by the responsible server mods such as NoxZ Phone, Expansion Market, trader or event logic. D-MarkZ contains no client-side money generation.
