[h1]👑 DEUTSCHZ KOTHZ — INFECTED SIEDGE V2[/h1]

[b]Capture the zone. Hold for five minutes. Survive five escalation stages and the BosZ.[/b]

DeutschZ KotH Infected Siedge v2 is a server-authoritative King-of-the-Hill PvPvE event featuring a long-range smoke signal, contestable capture zone, five infected waves, a 7,500-HP BosZ and story rewards for the next event stage. Version 0.4.5-eventruntime addresses the reported flag, mast, movement, loot and cleanup problems.

[list]

[*] white smoke before capture

[*] red smoke only after genuine victory and BosZ death
[*] 300-second capture without multi-player acceleration
[*] five waves at 0 / 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 percent

[list]
[*] capture radius: [b]25 metres[/b]

[*] additional team members do not accelerate progress
[*] hostile players block capture

[h2]🧟 FIVE INFECTED WAVES[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]0%:[/b] 3 infected with 250 HP each
[*] [b]20%:[/b] 2 infected with 400 HP each
[*] [b]40%:[/b] 2 infected with 600 HP each; running disabled
[*] [b]60%:[/b] 3 infected with 800 HP each; running disabled
[*] [b]80%:[/b] 2 infected with 1,000 HP each, red eyes; running disabled
[/list]

Walk-only waves now use the valid Vanilla movement stage [b]1[/b] instead of the invalid value 0.75. This is intended to prevent infected from freezing at 60 percent. The BosZ mummy also walks faster while remaining unable to run.

[list]

[b]4. DEFEAT BOSZ[/b] — only the genuine boss victory enables red smoke and the reward phase.

[b]5. SECURE STORY ITEMS[/b] — the new punched-card/keycard appearance and matching card reader support the next event stage.
