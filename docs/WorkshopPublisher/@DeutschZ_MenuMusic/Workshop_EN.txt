[h1]🖥️ DEUTSCHZ MENÜZ — MENU, LOADING, MUSIC, DEATHSCREEN & SPAWN[/h1]

[b]Your DeutschZ experience begins before you enter Chernarus — and does not end at the deathscreen.[/b]

DeutschZ MenüZ combines the main menu, loading screen, music, deathscreen and spawn interface in one client/server module. Its visual direction follows the dark DeutschZ gaming style and completely avoids spatial speaker objects and trader speakers.
